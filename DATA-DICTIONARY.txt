RENT-BURDEN CSV FIELD GUIDE — ACS2024
===================================

Original analysis dictionary by Ahmadreza Samadi: https://a-samadi.com/about.html
AI-assisted preparation; this is an analysis companion, not Census certification.

Scope: 386 selected whole metros; 35 columns. Source year 2024 is historical.
This is separate from the earlier 393-metro HUD rent/ownership study.

READ BEFORE COMPARING
- rent_30plus_pct and rent_50plus_pct use computable_units, excluding not computed.
- not_computed_pct_all_renters uses renter_units_total, including not computed.
- All three CSV percentage columns use 0–100, not 0–1. Do not apply a spreadsheet
  percent format to 57.8 without first dividing by 100; it would display 5780%.
- The 50% group is inside the 30% group. Never add their shares.
- Blank means unavailable, not zero. Negative source sentinel tokens are not counts.
- The 11 M columns are source-count margins of error, not derived-percentage errors.
  No significance ranking is established.
- Keep geo_id/cbsa_code as strings; do not infer equivalence with another vintage.

FIELDS IN CSV ORDER
-------------------
geo_id | string | identifier
Exact GEO_ID from the source geography file; keep as text, including its geography-vintage marker.

cbsa_code | string | identifier
Five-character CBSA code from the source geography file; preserve text and leading zeros.

metro_name | string | label
Published whole metropolitan-area label, not a property or city-only boundary.

survey_year | integer | year
ACS 1-year source year, not download year or current market year.

status | string | status
Analysis availability status. This fixed released snapshot contains only ok. The builder can also report unavailable source estimate or zero computable denominator.

renter_units_total | integer_or_blank | housing units
Total renter-occupied housing units; not a count of people or only cash-rent units.
Formula: B25070_E001

not_computed_units | integer_or_blank | housing units
Units for which the source ratio is not computed; retained separately, not treated as zero burden.
Formula: B25070_E011

computable_units | integer_or_blank | housing units
Denominator for the two threshold percentages.
Formula: B25070_E001 - B25070_E011

rent_30plus_units | integer_or_blank | housing units
Units in the published 30.0%-or-more bins.
Formula: B25070_E007 + B25070_E008 + B25070_E009 + B25070_E010

rent_50plus_units | integer_or_blank | housing units
Units in the published 50.0%-or-more bin; a subset of rent_30plus_units.
Formula: B25070_E010

rent_30plus_pct | number_or_blank | percent (0–100)
Share of computable units at 30.0% or more, rounded to six decimals; not a 0–1 fraction.
Formula: 100 * rent_30plus_units / computable_units
Blank if: computable_units is zero or source estimate unavailable

rent_50plus_pct | number_or_blank | percent (0–100)
Share of computable units at 50.0% or more; nested inside the 30% group. Do not add these percentages.
Formula: 100 * rent_50plus_units / computable_units
Blank if: computable_units is zero or source estimate unavailable

not_computed_pct_all_renters | number_or_blank | percent (0–100)
Share of all renter-occupied units with a not-computed ratio; its denominator differs from the two burden percentages.
Formula: 100 * not_computed_units / renter_units_total
Blank if: renter_units_total is zero or source estimate unavailable

MACHINE-READABLE DICTIONARY AND CHECK
------------------------------------
data-dictionary.json preserves exact source labels and all 35 columns in order.
It uses a small custom JSON format; no external schema-standard conformity is claimed.
Run from the repository root: python verify_dictionary.py
The checker validates this fixed 2024 snapshot: header, file hashes, source labels,
386 unique rows and all derived counts/percentage relationships.
It does not recalculate uncertainty, test Excel, or prove correct future vintages.

For source selection, gross-rent definitions, reuse exceptions and citation,
read README.md and CITATION.cff; retain attribution to the underlying Census table.
Data values, chart and workbook are unchanged by this documentation companion.
